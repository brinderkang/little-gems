About Little Gems

Welcome to Little Gems Childcare

Welcome to Little Gems Childcare, a warm and nurturing early learning centre where every child is treasured, valued, and supported to shine. We provide education and care for children 2 to 6 years old, offering a safe, supportive, and home-like environment where tamariki can learn and grow.

Located in the heart of Papakura, Auckland, we offer a play-based curriculum that supports children's natural curiosity and love of learning. Our experienced educators are passionate about early childhood education and dedicated to helping each child reach their full potential.

We believe that each child is a "Little Gem" with unique strengths, interests, and potential — and it is our privilege to help them grow.

Our Teaching Philosophy

Our teaching philosophy is guided by Te Whāriki, the New Zealand Early Childhood Curriculum, emphasising belonging, wellbeing, contribution, communication, and exploration. Through playful learning, creativity, music, outdoor experiences, and hands-on activities, we encourage tamariki to explore, question, and discover the world around them.

We celebrate culture, identity, and diversity, embracing the rich values of te ao Māori and weaving them into our daily practices. Our centre is a place where everyone—children, families, and staff—feels welcomed, respected, and valued.

At Little Gems, we nurture children's confidence, spark their curiosity, and help them build strong foundations for lifelong learning.

Why Choose Little Gems?

📚

Age-Appropriate Curriculum (2-6 Years)

Our curriculum supports every stage of development, sparks curiosity, encourages independence, and helps build essential social, emotional, and cognitive skills. With small group learning, each child receives personalised attention to thrive and grow.

🏡

Home Away From Home

A warm, welcoming, and nurturing environment where every child is treated with aroha and respect and feels a true sense of belonging. We foster strong whānau partnerships, encouraging open communication and involvement.

🌳

Outdoor Learning & Play

Our outdoor environments are safe, fun, and challenging, designed to promote exploration, physical activity, and confidence. Children develop gross motor skills, teamwork, problem-solving, and a love for nature as they play and learn outdoors.

👩‍🏫

Experienced & Passionate Kaiako

Our experienced kaiako are deeply committed to nurturing each child's growth, inspiring their curiosity, building confidence, and helping them reach their full potential.

🌍

Celebrating Culture & Diversity

"At Little Gems we are one family with many cultures. We embrace inclusive practices that celebrate te ao Māori, individual identity, and the rich diversity of cultures around us. We use culture as a teaching tool, recognising and celebrating events from all cultural backgrounds. This helps our tamariki develop a deeper understanding of their own culture while learning to appreciate and respect the cultures of others."

Together We Grow Your Precious Gems — Ka Tupu Ngātahi
